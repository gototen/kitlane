# Invitation and messages

Plain text to paste into email or chat. Replace the bracketed parts. Every message is worded so it never mentions the rail, the drawer, recommendations, or other tools. Don't add hints when you personalize them.

## Invitation (D−5 to D−3)

> Subject: Would you try a small tool for a few days?
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I'm building Kitlane, an open-source place for teams to share the small web tools they make. I'd like to see how it feels in real use, and you'd be a big help.
>
> What it involves:
>
> 1. Sign in with your Google account at https://kit.kitlane.dev/focus. It's a simple focus timer.
> 2. Use it when it suits you on a couple of days this week, whenever you'd want a timer for a stretch of work. Nothing to prepare.
> 3. On [day/date] we have a 20–30 minute call (or I come by) where I watch you do a short task with it. I'm testing the software, not you.
> 4. About a week later I'll send you two or three quick questions.
>
> Please don't type anything real or confidential into it; it's a demo with sample content.
>
> What gets recorded: the demo logs which tools you open and when, and how you move around it, but never what you type. During the study I can see those records for your account, so I can ask you about them afterwards. Anything I share from the study is aggregated and never names you. Full details are at https://kitlane.dev/privacy.html. You can drop out at any time, and I'll delete your data if you ask.
>
> Does [day/date] work?
>
> Thanks,
> [your name]

## Confirmation after they first sign in

> Thanks, you're in. Use the timer whenever it helps over the next few days. See you on [day/date] at [time].

## Session reminder (D−1)

> Quick reminder about our session tomorrow at [time]. Please have https://kit.kitlane.dev/focus open in your usual browser, signed in. [Call link / place.]

## Follow-up (D8)

Send it eight days after the session, and send it even if the report shows no activity.

> Hi [name], thanks again for last week. Three quick questions, and short answers are perfect:
>
> 1. Since our session, have you opened anything on the Kitlane demo? If so, what?
> 2. Was any of it useful for something you were actually doing? What?
> 3. Anything that confused or annoyed you?

If the report shows they reached a tool through the rail during the session or the familiarity days, you can add one specific question *after* their answers come back, never in the first message:

> You opened [tool title] at one point. Had you come across it before then, or anything like it?

## Close (after the last follow-up)

> The study is finished, and thank you. I'll [reset the demo / remove your data] on [date] unless you'd like to keep using it. If you're curious, I'm happy to share the aggregated results.
